ERGO CONNECT – SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (V1)
1. Project Overview
1.1 Project Name
ERGO Connect
1.2 Purpose
ERGO Connect is a digital collaboration platform connecting ERGO teams, ERGO customers, and ERGO suppliers across the sourcing and supply lifecycle.
1.3 Objectives
Create a single source of truth for RFQs, quotes, NPD, OFP, and quality activities
Improve transparency between ERGO, customers, and suppliers
Strengthen process discipline, quality, and commitment tracking
Reduce dependence on scattered communication channels
Enable scale through defined workflows, audit trails, and role-based access

8. Full Access Control Philosophy
Use strict role-based access control plus record-based restrictions.
8.1 Core Principles
Customer users can see only records belonging to their own company
Supplier users can see only records belonging to their own company
Supplier users cannot see competitor information
Customer users cannot see non-selected supplier quotations
ERGO internal roles are function-based and should see only the modules relevant to their role, except ERGO Management, ERGO Admin, and ERGO Key Account Manager as defined below
ERGO Key Account Manager can see all records related to assigned customers and linked suppliers/projects where needed for coordination
Any approval-sensitive function must be separately permission-controlled even if the user has edit access
Internal notes, margins, supplier ratings, and internal comparison logic must remain hidden from customers and suppliers unless explicitly marked shareable
8.2 Record-Based Visibility Rules
Customer-side visibility
Customer RFQ: only own company RFQs and quote responses shown to customer
Customer NPD: only own company NPD projects, sample status, milestones, and customer-side NPD actions
Customer OFP: only own production and order follow-up records
Customer Quality: only own complaints, approvals, and quality records that are customer-facing
Customer Key Account Manager: full visibility for that customer account across RFQ, NPD, OFP, Quality, Documents, Communication
Supplier-side visibility
Supplier RFQ: only RFQs assigned to that supplier
Supplier NPD: only NPD projects, activities, readiness items, and submissions linked to that supplier
Supplier OFP: only orders and production follow-up linked to that supplier
Supplier Quality: only quality records, NCRs, CAPA, and inspection items linked to that supplier
Supplier Key Account Manager: full visibility for that supplier account across assigned RFQ, NPD, OFP, Quality, Documents, Communication
ERGO-side visibility
ERGO RFQ: RFQ and quote-related modules, linked documents, linked communication
ERGO NPD: NPD projects, linked documents, linked communication, relevant supplier/customer interactions
ERGO OFP: production follow-up, order tracking, dispatch tracking, linked documents, linked communication
ERGO Quality: inspection, NCR, CAPA, deviation, quality dashboards, linked documents
ERGO Key Account Manager: full customer-account visibility for coordination, status review, communication, and escalation
ERGO Management: full visibility across all modules, dashboards, reports, approvals as configured
ERGO Admin: full system access including user creation, role mapping, masters, workflow settings, audit logs, and permissions configuration
9. Role Definitions
This section defines the intended responsibility and access behavior of each role. These role definitions should be treated as the governing logic for permissions, UI visibility, workflow actions, and data restrictions.
9.1 ERGO Internal Roles
ERGO Admin
Full system administrator
Creates and manages users, roles, permission mapping, workflow settings, master data, and system policies
Has visibility across all companies, records, and audit logs
Can override locks and re-open records where policy allows
ERGO Management
Executive visibility across all modules
Reviews dashboards, approves major business decisions, and monitors performance
Should not do routine transaction entry unless specifically allowed
Can approve sensitive workflows according to configured approval matrix
ERGO Key Account Manager
Customer-facing coordination role for assigned accounts
Full visibility to assigned customer records and linked supplier/project records relevant for coordination
Can review progress, communicate, escalate, and support alignment between customer, ERGO, and supplier
Should not access unrelated accounts
ERGO RFQ
Manages customer RFQs, supplier RFQ float, quote collection, comparison, and submission to customer
Controls supplier quote edit permission and quote validation
Responsible for commercial flow until quote closure
ERGO NPD
Owns project conversion into NPD, development milestones, activity tracking, and sample submission readiness
Coordinates supplier development actions and customer-facing NPD progress where required
Responsible for closure of NPD stage and handover to OFP
ERGO OFP
Owns production follow-up, delivery tracking, dispatch readiness, shipment progress, and order execution visibility
Coordinates between supplier, quality, and customer on live order commitments
ERGO Quality
Owns inspections, NCR, CAPA, deviation control, quality approvals, and quality-related dashboard visibility
Controls quality-related stage gates and closure conditions
9.2 Customer Roles
Customer RFQ
Creates and manages own RFQs
Reviews only ERGO-shared quotations
Can approve, reject, or request revision on quote proposals shown to own company
Customer NPD
Reviews sample status, NPD milestones, and customer-facing development records
Can provide sample-related comments, milestone acknowledgment, and approval where needed
Sees only own company projects
Customer OFP
Reviews order fulfillment, dispatch progress, shipment status, and customer-facing delivery records
No access to internal supplier-side commercial or competitor information
Customer Quality
Reviews complaints, sample approvals, customer-facing quality records, and closure items requiring customer confirmation
Can approve or reject customer-side quality acceptance where workflow requires it
Customer Key Account Manager
Full account-level visibility for own company across RFQ, NPD, OFP, Quality, Documents, and Communication
Acts as coordinator and escalation owner from customer side
Customer Management
Optional broader customer-side management role if the customer wants a supervisory user above functional roles
Can be configured later if required; for MVP it may remain inactive unless needed by a specific customer
9.3 Supplier Roles
Supplier RFQ
Receives assigned RFQs, submits quotations, raises clarification requests, and regrets RFQs with reason where needed
Can edit quotations only when ERGO explicitly opens edit permission
Supplier NPD
Updates supplier-owned NPD tasks, readiness, attachments, process confirmations, and issue responses
Sees only NPD records assigned to that supplier
Supplier OFP
Updates order status, production readiness, quantities, dispatch readiness, and blockers for assigned orders
Sees only supplier-owned live order records
Supplier Quality
Updates supplier-side inspection responses, NCR responses, CAPA, and quality documents linked to that supplier
Cannot approve final customer-side quality closure unless specifically configured
Supplier Key Account Manager
Full visibility across that supplier’s assigned RFQ, NPD, OFP, Quality, Documents, and Communication
Acts as commercial/coordination owner on supplier side

11. Special Rules and Exceptions
11.1 Visibility Exceptions
Customer users must never see competitor supplier data or ERGO internal comparison notes
Supplier users must never see competitor supplier data, customer target price, ERGO margins, or internal ranking logic
ERGO Key Account Manager sees cross-functional data only for assigned accounts, not for all global data unless separately authorized
11.2 Edit Exceptions
Supplier quotation edit is allowed only when ERGO explicitly opens quote edit permission
Customer and supplier document edits are limited to files uploaded by their own company and only until the document is locked or approved
Communication edit is limited to own messages/comments until workflow or response lock applies
11.3 Approval Exceptions
Quality-related approvals may require ERGO Quality and, where applicable, Customer Quality approval
Management approval is not required for every transaction; it should apply only to configured high-risk or high-value workflows
NPD conversion is manual by ERGO only, but the system should show approved projects ready for conversion in the NPD section
11.4 Coding Notes
Access should be controlled at both module level and record level
Action buttons must appear only if user has permission for that action
Supplier quote edit button should appear only when ERGO toggles quote edit permission to open
Customer quote approval action should appear only for ERGO-shared selected quotes
Internal notes must have separate visibility flag from external comments

This section defines the core workflow logic needed to drive status transitions, approvals, and restricted actions.
14.1 Workflow Design Principles
Every business-critical object should have both a status and a workflow history
Status changes must be role-controlled
Approval flows should be configurable, not hardcoded
Re-open actions should require reason capture
Rejection and send-back actions should require comments
Workflow transitions should trigger notifications automatically
