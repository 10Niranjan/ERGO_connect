ERGO Connect · prepared for ERGO · 16 November 2026

What this is. You asked for new screens to register customers and suppliers. The screens should cover everything needed to judge a company on financial, process and quality grounds, with audit reports that management reviews before approving the company. Before we build them, we need your answers to the questions below.

How to answer. Tick one option per question, or write your own answer on the line. Each question shows our suggestion; if you agree with all of them, simply tick "Agree" in the Quick answer sheet on the last page.
