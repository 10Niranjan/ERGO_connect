Pilot Test Plan: RFQ to Closure

ERGO Connect · prepared for ERGO · 18 November 2026

What this is. Before customers and suppliers start using ERGO Connect for real work, a small group of real people should run one part through the whole process on the live system: request, quotation, sample, production, delivery and closure. This is the last open item on the go-live checklist. The pilot shows whether the screens, the hand-offs between people and the "who sees what" rules work for your teams, and it gives us a list of anything to fix.

Who takes part. People from one customer company, one supplier company and ERGO, each in their own role (Section B). One pilot coordinator at ERGO keeps the master copy of this document, ticks the results and collects the issues.

How to use this document

  • Each step names who does it, what to do and what to check. Do the step, compare with "Check", then tick Pass or Fail.
  • If a step fails or something looks wrong, write it in the Issue log (Section G) with the step number, then ask the coordinator whether to carry on.
  • Each step takes a few minutes. The whole run takes several days, because each person acts in turn and the system tells the next person in the app (the bell icon) when it is their turn. Ask everyone to check their bell daily during the pilot.
  • Button and menu names are shown in bold blue. The "Who" column names the role, for example Customer RFQ is the customer's RFQ user.

Ground rules. The pilot runs on the live system, so its records stay in the database. Start every title and part number with PILOT (for example part PILOT-001) and use only files that are not confidential. Everyone signs in with their own account and never shares a password. Do not use the pilot accounts for real business until the coordinator says the pilot is closed.
