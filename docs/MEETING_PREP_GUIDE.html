Part 1

Understand it yourself

Tonight · about 1 hour
The project in one lineERGO Connect is one website where ERGO, its customers and its suppliers take a part from “how much will this cost?” to “delivered and closed”.

The easy way to remember it: ERGO is like a travel agent

A customer asks the agent for a price. The agent asks the hotels, adds its own margin and gives the customer its own price. The customer never deals with the hotel, and the hotel never knows who the customer is. Here it is the same:

Remember just 3 things

1
3 types of company

ERGO sits in the middle. The customer needs parts. The supplier makes them.

2
The golden rule

Customer never sees the supplier's name or price. Supplier never sees the customer, the target price, other quotes or ERGO's margin.

3
A journey in 3 stages

Price → Sample → Production. Every step has an owner, and nothing moves on without the right approval.

The journey in 3 stages

Stage A
Price
“How much?”
  1. Customer sends an RFQ (many parts, files per part)
  2. ERGO reviews it and sends parts to a supplier
  3. Supplier quotes its price
  4. ERGO makes its own quotation (with margin)
  5. Customer approves, per part
Stage B
Sample
“Can you make it right?”
  1. Customer raises a Sample PO
  2. ERGO KAM creates the project
  3. Supplier makes a plan, ERGO agrees
  4. Work is done step by step
  5. Quality check by ERGO and supplier
  6. Customer approves the sample
Stage C
Production
“Make the real quantity”
  1. Customer releases a Production PO
  2. KAM creates the production project
  3. Order: material → production → inspection
  4. Dispatch → delivered → closed
⛔ Dispatch is blocked until the inspection passes

Part 4

Questions, rules and logins

Questions they will probably ask

Can a supplier find out who the customer is?
No. It sees a neutral number, also in notifications and search.
Can the customer see the supplier's name or price?
No. It sees only ERGO's own quotation.
Does the supplier learn whether the customer approved?
No. It only sees “Accepted”.
Can one RFQ go to several suppliers?
Yes. Parts can be split between suppliers; each part is with one supplier at a time.
Can goods ship without an inspection?
No. Dispatch is blocked until the inspection passes.
Can one person have several roles?
Yes. Pick one at login and switch any time; one role at a time.
Who changes what a role can do?
Only the ERGO Admin, from a screen. A reason is required and every change is kept in the history.
Can the Admin approve things?
No. Approvals stay with the business roles.
Can the history be edited or deleted?
No, not even by the Admin.
Do we get emails?
Today notifications are in the app's bell. Email is only for password reset and the login code. Want more? Note it as a request.
Where is our data?
On a cloud server, with an encrypted backup every night.
When can we start using it?
After a short pilot. We need the pilot users, master data and the domain (next steps 1–3).
