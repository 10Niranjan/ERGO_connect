You chose: "1st option with running RFQ also stop". So ERGO Management suspends a company with a reason, no new RFQs or orders can start for it, and its running RFQs stop too. These details decide exactly what "stop" does.

On hold: nobody can work on it; it continues where it stopped when the suspension is lifted.
Cancelled: it is closed for good, with the suspension as the reason.
Our suggestion: on hold, so nothing is lost if the suspension is lifted.

Only that supplier stops: it cannot quote any more, its quotes cannot be selected, and the RFQ carries on with the other suppliers.
The whole RFQ stops, for every supplier.
Our suggestion: only that supplier stops. The customer is not held up by one supplier's problem.
